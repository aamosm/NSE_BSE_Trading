READ THE ENDING BEFORE BEGINNING:
Continue the existing NSE/BSE cash-research project.

Read the latest local HANDOFF, DATA_READINESS, FABLE_HANDOFF,
RESEARCH_BRIEF, FABLE_MATH_SPEC, current cost summary and Forecast
interface. The latest milestone report supersedes older status claims.

Do not rebuild infrastructure, edit the main repository, run tests,
place orders, purchase services or invent missing historical data.
This session is a design specification for the missing forecasting,
allocation and research-orchestration layers.

Retain useful existing interfaces. Identify exactly what is reused,
extended and new. Resolve conflicting old deliverable instructions
in favour of design-only work for this session.

OPERATING MODEL

The initial deployment is the owner's Windows laptop, not a VPS.
All schedule times use Asia/Kolkata, regardless of machine timezone.

Proposed normal-day schedule:
- 08:00: start and manual official Kite authentication.
- 08:05–08:15: account/data/rules/calendar health checks.
- 08:15–08:45: overnight research and morning summary.
- 09:00–16:15: continuous collection and monitoring.
- 16:15–16:45: reconciliation, available closing data and summary.
- Optional 18:30–19:00: late filings and delayed data.
- Separate bounded weekend research jobs.
the timings and other parts of this entire prompt are suggestions. if you find better implementations or find these to be wrong and think your idea is more accurate predictor. then go ahead and do so and explain as well.
Treat these as operating windows, not exchange-session definitions.
Use the existing dated exchange calendars and instrument-specific
session rules, including auctions, special sessions and holidays.

Do not assume the funds-addition delay from 00:00 to 07:30 defines
the availability of every Kite endpoint. Require fresh confirmed
account data before cash-dependent proposals.

Design startup, late login, laptop sleep, lost internet, maintenance,
token expiry, missed jobs and restart behaviour. Preserve last good
data with explicit age; unknown values must not become zero.

Catch-up downloads must not create fictional past decisions or fills.
Missing intraday depth/ticks cannot be reconstructed from daily bars.
Keep actual account observations separate from paper accounting.

FORECASTING PRIORITY

Specify company-event forecasting over multi-session horizons first,
supported by financials, price response, liquidity and market context.

Compare:
A. Prices/liquidity/context.
B. A plus financials and official events.
C. B plus news/attention.

Keep longer-term company selection extensible. Intraday and first-day
IPO strategies require separate data/execution readiness.

Define labels, fitting, calibration, uncertainty, abstention, exits,
whole-share allocation, shared cash and concentration constraints.
Use the existing forecast/rules/cost contracts wherever suitable.

RESEARCH SERVICE

Separate:
1. Direct collection from known official sources.
2. Scheduled and event-triggered targeted search.
3. Optional bounded deep-research jobs.

The candidate combined search/extraction provider is Perplexity Agent
API, subject to verifying model/tool/schema/filter compatibility.
Keep the provider replaceable; do not invent endpoint capabilities.

Start with a proposed $5–10 monthly runtime search/extraction ceiling.
This is separate from the Fable development budget.

Define job priority, daily/monthly limits, tool-call and token limits,
timeouts, deduplication, caching and exhaustion behaviour.
Enforce limits in software; a sentence in a prompt is not a budget cap.

Use exchange, regulator and company IR sources for factual events.
Use a separately approved publisher set to measure news attention.
Do not count syndicated copies as independent confirmations.

Create versioned prompt templates for:
- Overnight updates.
- Upcoming-event verification.
- Material-event investigation.
- Contradiction resolution.
- Bounded deeper company research.

Each template must receive explicit company identifiers, aliases,
time window, task, allowed sources, requested fields and known facts.
It must request new evidence and contradictions, not a buy/sell opinion.

Provide the JSON Schema and validation rules. Include event identity,
publication/receipt/effective times, amounts and units, source URLs,
supporting passages, novelty, contradictions and unknown fields.

Require retrieval evidence for citations; validate returned domains
and redirects. Treat retrieved text as untrusted data, not instructions.
No credentials, private account details or trading tools go to the LLM.

REPRODUCIBILITY AND SOURCE SCORING

Do not assume fresh LLM/search calls reproduce previous output.

Archive source evidence where permitted, hashes, search results,
extractions, model identifiers, prompts, schema versions and usage.
Record retention limitations where source terms restrict archiving.

Historical replay consumes saved point-in-time evidence and accepted
facts. It must not search today's internet or substitute newer facts.

Separate factual source reliability, extraction accuracy and calibrated
market probabilities. Do not use self-rated AI confidence as a trading
probability or update source trust based simply on trading profit.

Specify how reviewed factual outcomes can update reliability estimates,
including small-sample uncertainty and minimum evidence requirements.
All predictive-weight changes happen in offline versioned research.

LATENCY AND SAFE OPERATION

The collector, numerical decision engine and research worker must not
block one another. Deep research is never in the critical decision path.

Specify freshness requirements by horizon and data type.
Record publication, receipt, extraction completion, decision and
simulated execution times.

A forecast may use a researched fact only after that fact was available
to this system. Late results cannot retroactively improve a decision.

Define asynchronous queues, bounded retries, rate limits, job expiry,
duplicate suppression and revalidation before accepting a proposal.

If required evidence is unavailable, choose WAIT/ABSTAIN.
Do not silently substitute stale information or fabricated fills.

During market hours, development and historical experiments must use
separate processes/data copies and resource limits. No hot replacement
of the active model or strategy.

DELIVER

1. A concise extension map against the existing project.
2. The forecasting and allocation mathematical specification.
3. A calendar-aware laptop operating/state-transition specification.
4. Research-job schemas, prompt templates and provider adapter contract.
5. Source/extraction reliability and reproducibility rules.
6. A cost/latency budget with explicit assumptions and measurements needed.
7. Function contracts and hand-worked numerical examples.
8. A frozen historical/prospective evaluation plan.
9. SEARCH_REQUESTS for GPT and a prioritized implementation backlog.
