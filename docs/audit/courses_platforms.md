# Courses, broker APIs and comparable websites

Research date: 6 November 2026, Asia/Kolkata. Scope is the user's confirmed `marketlab-integrity` cash-equity research and paper-investing system. Sources were public pages and read-only project documents; no broker credentials, paid subscriptions, orders, or login automation were used. Public page evidence is in `evidence/courses_platforms_pages.json` with retrieval times and SHA-256 hashes. Main-project files can change while the other agent works; observations here are a read-time snapshot rather than an assertion about later revisions.

## What can the requested courses teach, and can their calculations be trusted?

### Takeaway

Use Unofficed as a project catalogue and introduction to Indian data access, but independently verify its formulas before reusing code. Two accessible sample lessons contain specific issues that make them unsuitable as calculation oracles. For this project, market mechanics, data provenance and validation are more valuable next lessons than adding more trading indicators.

### Cited Findings

- The requested [Unofficed Blackbox](https://unofficed.com/blackbox/) page is a resource directory: NSEPython and Kite courses, scanner exercises, paper-bot/index-construction projects, backtesting workshops, and links to repositories. It also links Selenium broker-login automation and options-focused projects. Those do not fit this project's manually authenticated, cash-only, read-only broker boundary. Blackbox is not evidence of a validated investment model or a single deployable trading engine. The source itself calls NSEPython an unofficial NSE API.
- The [NSEPython beginner course](https://unofficed.com/courses/mastering-algotrading-beginners-guide-nsepython/) is listed as free, with login to enroll. Its public contents cover plotting, historical data, beta, alpha, Sharpe, ADF, drift, volatility, GBM and option Greeks. I read the public listing and accessible Sharpe/beta sample lessons; I did not complete the enrolled course. Options Greeks have little immediate relevance to this cash-equity scope.
- **Confirmed unit error in the public Sharpe example:** the [Sharpe lesson](https://unofficed.com/courses/mastering-algotrading-beginners-guide-nsepython/lessons/use-python-to-calculate-the-sharpe-ratio-for-a-stock/) subtracts `risk_free_rate = 10` from returns derived from `pct_change()` while describing that input as 10%. The final helper repeats the mismatch. Decimal returns require 0.10 for 10%. Its displayed −27.06 example is therefore not a reliable stock-performance conclusion. The helper also compounds the mean daily return, which is neither the sample CAGR nor the same arithmetic annual-return convention used earlier. These are source-code observations, not a claim to have rerun the lesson on current SBIN data. [Pandas documents fractional, rather than percentage-point, output](https://pandas.pydata.org/docs/reference/api/pandas.Series.pct_change.html).
- **Chronology/alignment problems in the beta example:** the [beta lesson](https://unofficed.com/courses/mastering-algotrading-beginners-guide-nsepython/lessons/how-to-find-the-beta-of-indian-stocks-using-python/) computes changes before an explicit ascending-date sort; its displayed sample dates descend. It pairs return lists by position rather than a date join, so a missing date can silently mismatch observations. The final helper also requests 255 calendar days and describes them as trading days. The covariance/variance identity is valid; the data preparation is not robust. Beta measures market sensitivity, not a general ratio of total volatility.
- The listed [GBM lesson URL](https://unofficed.com/courses/mastering-algotrading-beginners-guide-nsepython/lessons/how-to-simulate-geometric-brownian-motion-in-a-stock/) redirected in the web tool to an unrelated product page. I did not treat that response as educational content or infer a security diagnosis. Individual course links need checking.
- [Zerodha Varsity](https://zerodha.com/varsity/) is free, public and requires no signup. Its market education is a sensible starting point for understanding delivery equity, corporate actions, trading mechanics and risk before coding calculations.
- [Quantra Python for Trading Basic](https://quantra.quantinsti.com/course/python-trading-basic) advertises a free introductory course covering Python, NumPy, Pandas and a buy-and-hold exercise. [Quantra Backtesting Trading Strategies](https://quantra.quantinsti.com/course/backtesting-trading-strategies) has a public syllabus and free introductory preview; I did not verify all paid content or completion outcomes. [Financial Time Series Analysis](https://quantra.quantinsti.com/course/financial-time-series-analysis-trading) covers stationarity and AR/MA/ARIMA/GARCH-type models. These are curriculum facts, not an endorsement of advertised strategy returns.
- The project's [START_HERE.md](C:/Users/d_hru/try/tradebot/marketlab-integrity/docs/START_HERE.md) and [STOCK_RANKING.md](C:/Users/d_hru/try/tradebot/marketlab-integrity/docs/STOCK_RANKING.md) already describe a more advanced system than the beginner examples: a 20-session benchmark-relative ridge forecast, bootstrap shrinkage, conformal outcomes, whole-share cost gates and a constrained allocator. It explicitly labels the model unvalidated. The separate calculation-audit notes examine actual code/results, rather than accepting documentation's correctness claims.

### Inferences

- Practical learning order: (1) Varsity market mechanics and corporate actions; (2) public Python/dataframe lessons; (3) official exchange and broker data contracts; (4) time-series validation and backtesting; (5) only then experiment with additional model families. Unofficed's scanner and index-fund projects are closer to the user's scope than BankNIFTY options or login automation.
- Any imported tutorial calculation should first pass known-value tests for decimal/percentage units, ascending dates, duplicate dates, joined benchmark timestamps, missing observations, zero variance, split handling and session counts. A course's worked output is not an independent oracle.
- A synthetic unit demonstration saved as `evidence/courses_platforms_math_demo.py` uses a 12% annual return, 10% annual risk-free rate and 20% annual volatility: consistent units produce Sharpe 0.1; substituting 10 produces −49.4. It also verifies beta 2 on date-aligned returns with a missing benchmark-only date. This is a demonstration of arithmetic, not a backtest or a stock recommendation.

### Gaps

- Enrollment-only material, coaching quality, learning outcomes and paid course completeness were not audited. Do not describe this as a full course review.
- No evidence established that the main project imports the flawed Unofficed helper. The repository/data audit should distinguish downloaded educational code from runtime dependencies.
- A daily Sharpe implementation must define risk-free conversion, arithmetic versus geometric reporting, sample standard deviation and annualization assumptions. A one-line formula cannot establish forecast skill or profitability.

## Is Zerodha better than the alternatives, and what is practical for Indian equities?

### Takeaway

For this specific system, retain the existing read-only Kite adapter for live quotes and missing historical candles, and keep official exchange files as the source for raw prices and historical universe/status. A free NSE wrapper complements this architecture; it does not replace a supported broker API. Upstox and FYERS merit a small data-quality comparison if subscription savings matter, but switching data sources will not fix model calibration or an empty selection gate.

### Cited Findings

| Provider | Verified capabilities and costs | Consequence for this project |
|---|---|---|
| Zerodha Kite Connect | Official [pricing](https://zerodha.com/products/api/) lists a free personal tier and ₹500/month Connect tier with live WebSocket and historical candles. [Historical docs](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/historical/) support OHLCV/OI and minute-to-daily intervals. | Most convenient continuity: it is already documented/configured locally. Keep SDK/order capabilities outside research code. |
| Kite limits | [Rate-limit docs](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/exceptions/) specify historical candles 3 requests/second and quotes 1/second. An older [staff forum table, edited 2020](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/3105/historical-api-frequency-and-limit-on-number-of-candles-fetched) lists 60 calendar days/request for minute candles and 2,000 for daily. | Chunk requests and record missing coverage. The older per-request table is not a guarantee of complete current history for every instrument; verify actual responses. |
| Upstox | [Current API product page](https://upstox.com/trading-api/) advertises free trading and data APIs; [charges page](https://upstox.com/brokerage-charges/?hl=en_IN) lists free API subscriptions. [V3 historical docs](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-historical-candle-data/) say minute/hour data from January 2022, daily/weekly/monthly from January 2000, with request-window limits depending on interval. | Strong cost-conscious second-source candidate. Account/authentication, actual symbol coverage, corporate adjustment policy and download limits still require validation; free API does not mean free trades. |
| DhanHQ | [Data API subscription](https://dhan.co/support/platforms/dhanhq-api/how-does-the-dhanhq-data-api-subscription-work/) is ₹499 plus taxes per 30 days. [Historical docs](https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/historical-data/) describe daily and minute OHLCV, five years of intraday data for active instruments, at most 90 days/request. [Support](https://dhan.co/support/platforms/dhanhq-api/is-the-historical-data-from-dhan-s-data-api-adjusted-for-corporate-actions-like-bonuses-and-splits/) confirms daily split/bonus adjustment. | Similar subscription cost to Kite, so no obvious cost-driven reason to switch. Useful for independent samples, subject to explicit adjustment checks. |
| FYERS | API access is advertised as free, but [current Standard/Prime limits](https://support.fyers.in/portal/en/kb/articles/what-are-the-fyers-api-rate-limits-for-standard-and-prime-plans) distinguish 5 years daily/1 year intraday on Standard from 20+ years daily/5+ years intraday on Prime. Standard Data API limits are 5/sec, 50/min, 5,000/day; Prime 10/sec, 500/min, 500,000/day. This expanded Prime offering was [announced 16 October 2026](https://fyers.in/whats-new/enhanced-api-benefits-with-fyers-prime). | Older claims of unlimited free deep FYERS history are stale. Five years of daily data can still be useful, but may not cover all warm-up/evaluation history. Prime price was not verified. |
| NSEPython / public wrappers | [Unofficed's own directory](https://unofficed.com/blackbox/) identifies NSEPython as unofficial and links local/server editions. | Good for exploration and public endpoint conveniences. No evidence of exchange service guarantees or a supported order interface; review endpoint stability, licensing and data quality separately. |

- **Kite adjustment semantics matter:** [current Zerodha support](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/charts-and-orders/charts/articles/kite-charts-not-matching-as-per-the-records-in-nse-or-bse) says historical prices account for bonus, split, rights, spin-offs and extraordinary dividends of at least 2% of the stock's market value. That is not a blanket ordinary-dividend total-return series. The current [staff response on historical data](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/15971/getting-the-right-closing-prices-adjusted-for-corporate-actions) says adjustments occur before market opening on the ex-date, volume is adjusted where relevant except dividends, and there is no dedicated corporate-action API. These are more specific than an older [2024 forum reply saying all corporate actions](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/14381/in-historical-data-are-prices-adjusted-for-splits-and-bonus) and supersede older weekend-only discussions.
- The active [KITE_AND_SERVER_SETUP.md](C:/Users/d_hru/try/tradebot/marketlab-integrity/docs/KITE_AND_SERVER_SETUP.md) already records provider-as-of-retrieval adjustment metadata and uses historical bhavcopies for universe membership, rather than today's Kite instrument list. It states an existing paid Kite plan; that account entitlement was not independently checked. [SOURCE_STATUS.md](C:/Users/d_hru/try/tradebot/marketlab-integrity/docs/SOURCE_STATUS.md) distinguishes unavailable historical depth from prospective quotes.
- Indian delivery equity is not zero cost even where brokerage is zero. [Zerodha's current schedule](https://zerodha.com/charges/) shows delivery STT 0.1% on each side, NSE transaction fees 0.00307%, stamp duty on purchase, SEBI fees, GST on service charges, and ₹15.34 DP per scrip on sale irrespective of quantity, subject to stated account-category exceptions/discounts. Fixed DP fees therefore matter proportionally more on small positions. This schedule must be date-versioned for historical replay rather than applied backward indiscriminately.
- [NSE market timings](https://www.nseindia.com/resources/exchange-communication-holidays) list regular equity trading 09:15–15:30 and separate pre-open, closing-auction/closing and block-deal sessions. The current page includes closing-auction timings; historical session logic must come from dated circulars and special-session calendars, not just a permanent weekday rule.
- [Zerodha's dedicated static-IP page](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/general-kite/kite-api/articles/static-ip) says API order placement requires a static IP from 1 April 2026, while WebSocket and other non-order APIs can be accessed from other IPs. The [SEBI September 2025 implementation circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/sep-2025/1759232056254.pdf) gives 1 April 2026 for application to all stock brokers. An [API FAQ](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/general-kite/kite-api/articles/kite-connect-api-faqs) inconsistently says April 2025; prefer the dedicated page and regulator circular. These order requirements are not a reason to add an order path to this read-only paper system.

### Inferences

- **Best architecture for the user's scope:** official dated exchange files for raw OHLCV, delisted/historical instruments, surveillance, bands and corporate events; one read-only broker adapter for needed quotes/history; normalized datasets with explicit raw versus adjusted series; reproducible local calculation and paper execution. A broker API is a data/execution transport, not a forecasting engine or validation method.
- Do not double-adjust Kite prices. Preserve retrieval date, provider and adjustment basis. Do not fill gaps in a raw-price series with provider-adjusted closes. Total return accounting must include dividends explicitly and align its benchmark definition.
- New broker evaluation should use the same small basket and dates, including a split, bonus, dividend, renamed/delisted security and missing session. Compare raw/adjusted conventions and continuity before downloading an entire universe. No claim here establishes that one broker has objectively superior price accuracy or uptime.
- NSE equity data and BSE data require separate symbol maps, calendars/rules and charges. Cash-only constraints still require surveillance/suspension/trade-to-trade awareness, tick sizes, whole-share affordability, delayed publication and conservative fill assumptions.
- The cost of the ₹500/month data subscription is not a per-trade exchange charge, but it belongs in an economic viability budget. At very small account sizes it may be larger than plausible expected trading gains; this does not justify tuning the model to create trades.
- A zero-buy screen can be a rational result of costs and uncertainty. Buying a different feed, adding indicators or relaxing gates until something passes is not validation. Separate rank order from qualification and paper authorization.

### Gaps

- No authenticated latency, completeness, entitlement or reconciliation benchmark was run across broker feeds. Public marketing uptime/speed claims were not treated as measured evidence.
- No complete ordinary-dividend adjustment policy for Upstox/FYERS was established here. Dhan's cited answer specifically verifies bonus/split daily adjustments, not all corporate actions or a total-return series.
- Market-data display/redistribution rights are not implied by public availability or API subscription. This is a private audit; a public product would need provider-specific permission review.

## Which existing websites already cover the calculations, and what remains custom?

### Takeaway

Yes: existing websites already cover screening, technical indicators, fundamental ratios and rule-based backtesting. Trendlyne is the closest documented comparison for a screened portfolio against a benchmark; Chartink and Streak are useful rule-screening comparators; TradingView is useful for single-asset indicator/strategy cross-checks. None of the inspected public documentation establishes an exact replacement for this project's cross-sectional forecast, calibrated uncertainty, capital-aware gates and immutable forward record.

### Cited Findings

| Website | What is documented | Fit and limitation relative to MarketLab |
|---|---|---|
| Chartink | [Scanner guide](https://chartink.com/articles/scanner/scanner-user-guide/) supports custom technical/fundamental scans. [Backtest FAQ](https://chartink.com/articles/backtest/backtest-results-faq/) warns that incomplete candles and mixed timeframes can repaint historical matches. | Fast independent check of deterministic filters. Historical matches are not proof of a fillable, after-cost portfolio strategy. Use completed-session snapshots when comparing. |
| Streak / Kite CTB | [Current Streak](https://streak.zerodha.com/) advertises scanners, equity/options strategies and backtests; [terms](https://streak.zerodha.com/terms) explicitly say order placement is manual and distinguish alerts from automated execution. [Kite CTB support](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/charts-and-orders/charts/articles/backtest-on-kite-charts) lists supported indicators/timeframes and says BSE is unsupported. | Useful no-code baseline for simple NSE rule strategies. Do not repeat older pricing or autotrading claims as current facts. |
| StockEdge | [Scans page](https://stockedge.com/scans) advertises 500+ predefined price, technical, fundamental and candlestick screens and combinations of up to ten. | Close competitor to a ready-made stock-screening dashboard; helpful for checking whether a screen idea is already commoditized. Score semantics and full replication of the custom research pipeline were not verified. |

[Broker emulator settings](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000786181-broker-emulator/) support commissions, slippage and fill assumptions. | Strong chart/indicator and single-stock strategy sanity check. - [MarketLab's documented ranking](C:/Users/d_hru/try/tradebot/marketlab-integrity/docs/STOCK_RANKING.md) combines benchmark-relative forecasts, bootstrap error/shrinkage, conformal outcomes, per-capital costs and allocation. Its [START_HERE.md](C:/Users/d_hru/try/tradebot/marketlab-integrity/docs/START_HERE.md) separates a top-expected-return list from the decision class and says the model is unvalidated. That distinction is central: an ordered list can always exist while no stock meets a buy gate.

### Inferences

- Use websites as bounded checks: same symbol, session, price adjustment basis, input window, timezone and formula; compare intermediate values and explain differences. Compare deterministic returns/volatility/ratios before attempting final rankings.
- Buy/build decision: if the goal is simply discovering stocks with technical/fundamental rules, an existing site may remove much maintenance. If the goal is an inspectable research experiment with honest uncertainty and reproducible forward evidence, the custom system has a distinct purpose. Its differentiation must be demonstrated by validation, not by how sophisticated its terminology sounds.
- The immediate useful addition is an audit view showing attrition by universe, history, exchange status, positive edge, downside gate and allocation; also raw forecast, shrinkage factor, cost and threshold distance. This addresses the user's frustration without manufacturing a recommended stock.
- Recommended comparator set for this project: Chartink for rule membership, Trendlyne for portfolio-screen functionality, TradingView for simple indicator calculations, Screener for fundamentals; Streak for a small predeclared NSE rule baseline. No purchases are required for the audit recommendation.

### Gaps

- Logged-in product workflows, paid backtest engines, adjustment databases and detailed numerical reproduction were not tested. Capability descriptions are supported by public documentation, not an end-to-end certification.
- No public source inspected establishes exact parity with the project's ridge, bootstrap, conformal calibration or capital-specific Indian cost accounting. Absence from these pages is not proof the platforms could never support a similar model.
- The main project's numerical correctness and testing-plan validity require the separate code/snapshot audit. These comparisons cannot resolve those questions by themselves.
